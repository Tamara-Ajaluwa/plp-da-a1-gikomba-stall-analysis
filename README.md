## Business Problem Summary
Wanjiku operates a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market. Over the past two weeks, she recorded daily sales and tracked basic stock inventory. This analysis evaluates daily sales trends, weekday versus weekend performance, and inventory reorder levels with gross margins to help guide her stock strategy and business growth.

## Top 3 Findings
1. **Weekend Revenue Surge:** Weekend daily sales average KES 4,900.00, outperforming weekday averages (KES 2,685.00) by 82.50%.
2. **Immediate Restock Required:** Avocados (87.50% margin), Tomatoes (72.22% margin), and Sukuma (50.00% margin) are at or below reorder levels and need urgent restocking.
3. **Positive WoW Growth:** Revenue grew 10.66% from Week 1 (KES 22,050.00) to Week 2 (KES 24,400.00).
